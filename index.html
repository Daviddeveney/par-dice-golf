How To Play

PAR Rules

Roll dice equal to par, make the match, and score what is left. Lowest total wins.

Learn It Fast

Make the match. Score the leftovers.

Par 3 wants a pair. Par 4 wants 3 of a kind. Par 5 wants at least 3 matching dice. Once you make the match, only the leftover dice count.

Par 5 starts with 4 rolls Straights add 1 bonus roll You can bank after any roll
Short walkthrough with quick checks.
Par 3 = pair Make a pair. The third die is the score.
Par 4 = trips Make 3 of a kind. The fourth die is the score.
Par 5 = 3+ match Make at least 3 matching dice. The other 2 score.

Turn Flow

Each hole works the same way

1 Roll the hole

Roll dice equal to par. Par 5 starts with 4 rolls.

2 Keep what helps

Keep the match, and keep low leftover dice when they help your score.

3 Bank or chase

Take the score showing, or reroll only the dice you did not hold.

4 Use bonus rolls

A straight adds 1 roll. You can still bank instead of using it.

Special Scores

Rare starts worth knowing

Par 3 hole in one 4, 4, 1 on roll 1 scores 1.
Par 4 albatross ace 2, 2, 2, 1 on roll 1 is Hole in One • Albatross.
Par 5 condor ace 3, 3, 3, 3, 1 on roll 1 is Hole in One • Condor.
All dice match later 5, 5, 5, 5, 5 after roll 1 is Birdie and scores 2.

Simple Strategy

Three habits help right away

Matches are worth zero

Once you make the match, stop worrying about those dice. Lower the leftovers.

Keep low leftovers

If a low die is already helping your score, protect it.

Know the miss rule

Par 3 and Par 4 misses use the lowest die. Par 5 misses count all 5 dice.

Bank good numbers

You do not have to use every roll. If you like the score, take it.

Quick Reference

The short version

  • Par 3 Pair + 1 scoring die. Miss = 3 + lowest die.
  • Par 4 3 of a kind + 1 scoring die. Miss = 4 + lowest die.
  • Par 5 3 of a kind + 2 scoring dice, with 4 base rolls.
  • Rolls 3 rolls to start, 4 on Par 5, plus straight bonus rolls
  • Best start Any first-roll score of 1 = Hole in One
  • Straights 3, 4, or 5 in sequence earn 1 bonus roll.
  • Goal Lowest total wins the round

Banking Tip

Ask one question

If I take this now, am I happy with the dice that still count?

  • Good bank You already built the match and the leftover dice are low.
  • Worth chasing You have the match, but one or two scoring dice can still improve a lot.
  • Free upside A straight gives you another look, but you never have to take it.
